Round 2 - Quiz

Instructions

You'll be presented with a series of multiple-choice questions. For each correct answer, you'll earn points, and an exciting twist is that your points will be multiplied by your answering streak. The longer you maintain a correct streak, the more points you can accumulate.


  • If you answer correctly, your current streak will increase.
  • If you answer a question incorrectly, your streak will reset to zero.
  • For each consecutive correct answer, your points for that question will be multiplied by your current answering streak.
  • After 4 consecutive correct answers, the next prompt becomes a 10x super question and your streak resets afterward.

Time Limit: 25 Minutes
