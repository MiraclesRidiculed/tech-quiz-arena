Round 1 - Hangman

Your first challenge is a computer-science themed hangman round. Reveal the hidden word before you run out of lives to unlock the quiz.


  • You start with 10 lives and guess one letter at a time.
  • Correct guesses reveal every matching letter in the word.
  • Wrong guesses remove a life and draw another part of the hangman.
  • You can use the hint button if you need an extra nudge.
  • Winning moves you to the quiz instructions page automatically.
